# Each search tool earns its place over bash

## Destination
A measured answer, from our own A/B runs against stock Pi, to whether `find`, `ls` and `grep` help or hurt current frontier models compared with bash, counting context cost and confusion. The answer becomes an owner-approved ruling, and each tool registers as that ruling says. Ships as 0.21.0.

## Out of scope
- Changing `read`, `edit`, `write` or `ast_search`: they are measured, not ruled on.
- Rerouting `grep`/`rg` commands into our grep implementation, as Claude Code embeds `bfs` and `ugrep` in its shell.
- The breaking removal of any tool: by the Nu precedent, it follows a published deprecation release, as a later arc.
- Publishing to npm: the owner's.
- Claims about model quality beyond the tested tasks and models.

## Decisions
- Owner, 2026-11-03 (chat): bloat is measured as context-window space and as dollar cost, separately. Every registered tool is measured, but only `find`, `ls` and `grep` are ruled on, each kept, slimmed, deferred or removed. The extension is judged for everyone who installs it, and any removal goes through a published deprecation. Confusion is the primary metric, measured by tool errors, redundant searches, wrong-tool picks and steps to the first useful hit, alongside tokens and pass rate, plus a qualitative trace review. The pilot runs under $20 on gpt-6-luna and one Claude model. The decision rule is slice 7's, and the owner approves the ruling before any tool changes. Anchored bash grep is conditional, as Not yet specified describes.
- Context, measured 2026-11-03: stock Pi 1.5.0 activates only `read`, `bash`, `edit` and `write` (`DEFAULT_TOOL_NAMES`). Claude Code 2.1.317 leaves Glob and Grep out by default on macOS, Linux and WSL, searching through bash with embedded `bfs` and `ugrep` ([tools reference](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/tools-reference)). mini-swe-agent scores >74% on SWE-bench Verified with bash alone. These are priors, not proof: none measured edit-ready anchors.
