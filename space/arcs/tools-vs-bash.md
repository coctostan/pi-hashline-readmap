# Each search tool earns its place over bash

This extension gives AI models its own file-search tools in Pi. We measure, against plain Pi, whether those tools help the models or only take up space, and whether plain shell search with edit-ready line marks could replace the search tool. Each tool is kept or put away by what we measure, in one release.

## Destination
A measured answer, from our own A/B runs against stock Pi, to whether `find`, `ls` and `grep` help or hurt current frontier models compared with bash, counting context cost and confusion, and whether anchored bash grep can replace the `grep` tool. The answers become owner-approved rulings, and each tool registers as they say. Ships as 0.21.0.

## Out of scope
- Changing `read`, `edit`, `write` or `ast_search`: they are measured, not ruled on.
- Rerouting `grep`/`rg` commands into our grep implementation, as Claude Code embeds `bfs` and `ugrep` in its shell, or rewriting them to add `-n`: anchored bash grep reads only what the command printed.
- The breaking removal of any tool: by the Nu precedent, it follows a published deprecation release, as a later arc.
- Publishing to npm: the owner's.
- Claims about model quality beyond the tested tasks and models.
